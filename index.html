Profile

I'm an 18-year-old student finishing school and heading into higher education in technology. Four years of Java, close to four years of building with AI tools every day, and a habit of turning ideas into working prototypes.

Most of what I care about sits where hardware, software and public safety meet. As Education Minister I speak for over a thousand students; as a builder I'd rather ship something that helps one person get home safely than another demo that looks good in a slide deck.

Right now that means Safeguard — a safety system designed around how emergencies actually unfold in India: seconds matter, networks are patchy, and the nearest help is usually another person, not a call centre.

  • Java since Class IX — four years of school-level programming
  • ~4 years of daily AI-assisted learning, research and building
  • English, Bengali, Hindi

I'd rather build the thing that wakes up a street than the thing that wins a pitch competition. Safety tech only counts if it works when someone is scared and alone.

Safeguard — personal safety, end to end.

Founder & Developer

One press arms the whole chain: a physical alarm, the live location pushed to everyone inside a radius, and an automatic call to emergency services.

  • 01

    ESP32 control board

    The brain: reads the button, drives the siren and hands the coordinates to the app.

  • 02

    GPS + antenna

    Locks the user's position so an alert carries a live pin, not a guess.

  • 03

    Siren & strobe

    A physical alarm and flashing light that pulls attention to the spot.

  • 04

    SOS button

    One press, no phone needed — it starts the entire sequence.

  • 05

    Companion app

    Alert radius, trusted contacts, and a live view of who responded.

Step 01
Trigger

One press on the module or app.

Step 02
Alarm

Siren and light draw attention on the spot.

Step 03
Broadcast

Live location goes to people nearby.

Step 04
Dispatch

112 is dialed automatically.
